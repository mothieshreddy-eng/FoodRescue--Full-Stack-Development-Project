How it works

Two sides, one short loop. Restaurants spend about a minute per post; organizations get a live view of what's nearby.

For restaurants

  1. Sign up onceAdd your FSSAI licence number, address and usual closing time.
  2. Post at closingDish, portions, veg or non-veg, and how long it can wait.
  3. Hand overCheck the volunteer's pickup code, hand over the food, done.
  4. See your totalA monthly summary of portions given, useful for CSR reports.

For food organizations

  1. Get verifiedShare your registration and the areas you can cover.
  2. Get alertsNew listings in your areas arrive by SMS or WhatsApp.
  3. Claim and collectReserve a listing, send a volunteer with the pickup code.
  4. Confirm deliveryMark it served so the restaurant knows where the food went.

Food safety comes first

Every listing follows FSSAI's surplus food rules (Recovery and Distribution of Surplus Food Regulations, 2019) and standard hot and cold holding guidance.

Above 60 °CHot food stays hot until pickup.
Below 5 °CCold and perishable items stay refrigerated.
2 hours maxPickup window for cooked food on the board.

Questions

Anything else, write to us.

What food can be posted?

Cooked meals that were never served, bakery items, fresh produce and sealed packaged food within its date. Plate leftovers and food that has been out of safe temperature can't be posted.

What happens if nobody claims a listing?

Listings close at the pickup deadline. If a listing is close to expiring, nearby organizations get a second alert.

Who does the pickup?

The organization that claims a listing sends its own volunteer or driver. Organizations without a vehicle can mark that at sign-up so we can pair them with volunteer drivers.

Do organizations have to be registered?

Yes. We verify each organization's registration before it can claim food, so restaurants know who they're handing it to.

Does it cost anything?

No. FoodRescue is free for restaurants and food organizations.
